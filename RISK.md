# Risk and execution boundary

Status: accepted product constraints; discrepancies in the current legacy schema are listed below. No real execution phase is authorized.

## Authority

LLMs may classify, challenge, summarize, rank, explain, and recommend one of a finite set of analysis paths. They receive immutable evidence; their output is untrusted advisory data. Confidence is not probability until calibrated against outcomes.

Only deterministic code may calculate or enforce position size, maximum leverage/exposure, risk budgets, liquidation thresholds, stop-distance risk, portfolio constraints, and account protection. A model's wording can never authorize those values or override a veto. Future human approval must bind a concrete proposal and evidence version; approval for analysis is not approval to trade.

For the present product there is **no account model, position-sizing service, execution engine, or trading authorization**. Do not construct them merely because the roadmap mentions risk. Start with a deterministic analysis-admissibility verdict (allow analysis / insufficient evidence / reject / stale), independent of a model recommendation. It must not be named an execution approval.

Current implementation correction (2026-09-30): the paragraph above predates the paper and pilot work and is no longer a complete description of the code. A paper game with the EX-1 paper exit policy and a paper position monitor, a deterministic Risk Engine with sizing, loss locks and a kill switch for a 240 EUR pilot shadow wallet, and a private Kraken adapter restricted to account reads now exist. The pilot shadow is a separate pretend EUR account in mode **PAPER**, not SHADOW_LIVE; it reads no real account value. The read adapter is disconnected from the radar, the loop, the paper game, the pilot and the UI; only the separate command `scripts/kraken_account_check.py` uses it, it requires an explicit `--mode SHADOW_LIVE`, and it refuses every endpoint outside its read allowlist before signing. There is still no live order path, no strategy has qualified, and no trading authorization exists. Every hard boundary below is unchanged. Paper wallet reporting adds reporting only: each paper wallet shows free cash, open cost basis, a conservative liquidation value, realized and open net P&L, each play's stored fee as ASSUMED (account tier unverified) and, for non-EUR pairs, that the EUR amounts exclude FX; `scripts/audit_paper.py` reads a paper database with `mode=ro` and never writes.

## Hard boundaries

- Public read-only Kraken endpoints only for the radar; no private API keys in the radar, transfers, order creation/cancellation, or leverage changes. The separate read-only account check above is the only code that calls a private endpoint, and only for reads. One scoped exception for public data: the research-only trend paper module also reads Binance public daily klines through its own GET-only adapter; see [Trend paper](#trend-paper-research-paper-only).
- No new cloud LLM calls, paid API dependency, or silent fallback. Development tooling is separate from the software's runtime inference.
- An unavailable quote, fee, depth, FX conversion, or account value remains unavailable. A partial cost total cannot be reported as a full net edge.
- Exact Decimal parsing belongs at new monetary boundaries; currency, instrument, contract type, units, and rounding policy travel with values. Existing float indicators may remain for statistical/ranking calculations. Do not cosmetically convert already-rounded SQLite REAL history into claimed exact money.
- Freshness gates run before expensive inference and before publication. A late result cannot be published as current because the model completed successfully.
- Free text, market symbols, and model content never become commands, SQL, tool names, or control-flow transitions without a typed, allowlisted deterministic interpreter.

## Current defects that must not propagate

`analysis_schema.py` currently permits `leverage`, `margin`, `notional`, `max_loss`, `stop`, and `net_rr` from Claude. These are legacy advisory outputs, not validated risk calculations. Retain historical rows as historical records; the future local analyst schema must not carry executable risk authority. Do not reuse this schema unchanged.

`tradeability.build_cost_preview()` labels fees UNCALIBRATED but adds missing spread/slippage as zero to the subtotal. It uses the maximum estimated buy/sell slippage, not a correctly specified two-leg cashflow calculation. Market selection currently favors futures when present; that does not establish execution suitability. No expected-profit claim is justified by this preview.

Sextant's `engine/risk` is a placeholder. Its risk verdict types and preflight tests are useful references, not a production risk engine or evidence that real account protection exists.

### Remaining risks

- **Every rule is NOT QUALIFIED.** 29 trials were counted (20 earlier trials plus 9 registered strategies in `docs/audit/2026-10-03-trend-feasibility/registry.jsonl`); no candidate passed both the development and the holdout split. `btc_trend5` and `btc_trend5_vt` lost money in the holdout year (pre-tax CAGR -3.9% and -1.2% at 0.1% fee plus 5 bps) while BTC buy-and-hold fell 29.7%; the `ens_btc` and `ens_vt_btc` holdouts are not clean evidence. Paper results are a forward observation and cannot qualify a rule; qualification would need a new pre-registered protocol and independent acceptance.
- **Paper fills are a model.** Fills at the public daily candle open with 0 slippage ignore the order book, partial fills and outages; BTC and ETH were chosen with hindsight.
- **Platform and regulatory risk (MiCA).** That Binance has no MiCA licence was reported (2026-10-03) and is not verified. A secondary press source reports that non-MiCA stablecoin spot pairs, USDT included, were delisted for EEA users, so the USDT books may not be executable for an EEA account; they are kept as the parity reference. The Kraken fee tier (0.4% maker, 0.8% taker) and Kraken's MiCA status were reported for the account and are not verified. Questions to Binance about dated futures and the notice period are unanswered, and the reported promotional fees may end.
- **Tax uncertainty.** Every paper figure is pre-tax; the paper books compute no tax. The research engine's Portugal model is approximate (28% on lots held under 365 days, FIFO, computed in USDT, not EUR). Whether a crypto-to-crypto exchange defers taxation or resets the holding period is UNVERIFIED and needs an accountant.
- **Data gaps and skipped days.** A candle counts as missing for good only when a later candle of the same pair exists and a second request agrees. A missing BTCEUR/ETHEUR open then falls back to the USDT open divided by the EURUSDT open (the source is recorded); if that is missing too, or a USDT candle is missing, the day is skipped (a chained skip line, books carried over, no alert). A permanent BTCUSDT gap skips every later day; a delisted EUR pair makes the catch-up wait. Kraken fills have no substitute price: a Kraken open missing for good is a skipped Kraken day, and because Kraken returns at most 720 daily candles, an older day is never confirmed and the Kraken catch-up waits. A PC clock behind the exchange fails with `CLOCK_SKEW` until corrected. Nothing runs while the PC is off or the radar is stopped; missed days are booked at the next start.
- **Ledger integrity is tamper-evident, not tamper-proof.** Removing whole trailing days or rehashing the whole file is not detected (the catch-up books removed days again from the same public candles). Readers take no lock. If the process ends during an append, the torn tail is refused until the ledger is renamed by hand as the guide describes.
- **Alerts are at most once.** Days booked by the CLI, by "Catch up now" or by a catch-up that failed after booking never alert, and a rebuilt ledger does not re-alert keys already in `alerts.jsonl`.
- **Frozen executable.** `CryptoRadarControlRoom.exe` was not rebuilt; the start hook runs only when the radar is started from source.

## Future risk gate, if separately authorized

Require explicit account/portfolio source, reconciliation timestamp, known equity and liabilities, instrument-specific margin rules, currency conversion provenance, deterministic sizing and exposure tests, and human approval. Unknown or stale account state fails closed. Until then use only synthetic/manual analysis scenarios clearly labeled as such; never invent account capital.

## Future design, explicitly separated from authority to execute

The user subsequently requested a complete trading end-state design. It is defined in `docs/EXECUTION_ARCHITECTURE.md`, with F0–F7 gates in `docs/FUTURE_TRADING_ROADMAP.md`. This closes the architecture; it does not change the current absence of account/private/order capabilities or authorize implementing/activating them now. The first possible live scope is cash-funded spot-long only under a concrete approved envelope and proven native protection.

Risk sizing consumes deterministic strategy stop/exit rules, exact account state/reservations, cost/FX/liquidity and instrument constraints. Advisory direction may influence a declared strategy only through the Trade Decision Engine policy. Missing mandatory data or an unresolved material objection can prevent entry; consensus cannot waive a veto. Stops and other hard protection continue without models, UI or Ollama. Native protection is mandatory but cannot guarantee a fill or loss bound during venue outage/gaps.
