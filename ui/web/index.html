Pilot shadow Pretend money - no orders - LONG only

A second pretend account that follows the same alerts with the limits planned for real trading. It only buys, holds one position at a time and sizes each entry so that a bad exit stays within its loss limit. It has no buttons: its kill switch and lock reviews are run from the command line.

Trend paper (research) Paper only — research, not qualified, no real orders

Four daily trend-following strategies on BTC and ETH (ENS, ENS_VT, btc_trend5 and btc_trend5_vt), each kept as a paper book from 2026-10-04 in EUR and in USDT, at a 0.1% and a 0.4% fee per leg. Prices come from public daily candles; no order is sent and no account is read. A research observation, not a qualified strategy.

Results are pre-tax: fees are charged on traded notional, slippage is 0 and no tax is deducted.

Words you will see

Bets it goes up / downThe AI wins if the price moves the way it picked before the play closes.
Stop (loss limit)A price fixed when the play opens, on the losing side, twice the coin's usual five-minute swing away. The play closes at the first real price that reaches it.
Target (profit goal)A price fixed when the play opens, on the winning side, twice as far away as the stop. The play closes at the first real price that reaches it.
Time limitA play that reaches neither the stop nor the target closes after a day at the latest. Nothing is moved or widened while it is open.
CommissionsWhat the exchange charges to buy and to sell. They are always paid, win or lose.
The gap between buying and selling priceThe exchange always sells a little above the price it buys at. A play buys at one and sells at the other, so the gap costs a little every time.
Pretend moneyNo order ever goes to the exchange. The prices are real; the money is not.
Before costsHow the price moved for the play, before the spread and the commissions are taken off.
Waiting for a priceThe closing time passed without a valid price; the play closes at the first valid one, and the delay is recorded.
